FACTORYZERO TEST DOCUMENT — MOTOR MAINTENANCE MANUAL
Document ID: FZ-MOTOR-MAN-001
Machine: MOTOR-REAL-001 / Production Drive Motor 001

NORMAL OPERATING REFERENCE
- Temperature: normally 55–80 °C.
- Vibration: healthy operation normally below 4.5 mm/s RMS.
- Current: typical operating current 20–27 A.
- Loaded speed: approximately 1770–1790 RPM.

WARNING / INVESTIGATION
- Temperature approaching or exceeding 80 °C requires investigation.
- Vibration from 4.5–7.0 mm/s RMS indicates a developing condition requiring inspection.
- Current approaching 30 A should trigger load and electrical checks.
- Persistent RPM reduction with rising current can indicate increased mechanical load.

CRITICAL / URGENT CONDITION
- Temperature above 85 °C with a rising trend requires urgent investigation.
- Vibration above 7.0 mm/s RMS requires prompt investigation.
- Sustained current above 30 A requires electrical/load investigation.
- High temperature + high vibration + rising current together indicate a potentially
  serious developing machine condition and should trigger controlled maintenance action.

Do not bypass overload protection or safety interlocks.
Follow the plant's approved LOTO procedure before maintenance.
