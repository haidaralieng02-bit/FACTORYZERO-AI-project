FACTORYZERO TEST DOCUMENT — SAFETY PROCEDURE
Document ID: FZ-SAFE-003

For any maintenance intervention:
1. Follow the site's approved Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedure.
2. Isolate electrical and mechanical energy sources.
3. Verify zero-energy state before opening guards.
4. Use required PPE and approved instruments.
5. Only qualified personnel may perform energized electrical measurements.
6. Never bypass overload relays, interlocks, guards, emergency stops, or protective devices.

For severe overheating, smoke, burning smell, exposed conductors, abnormal mechanical noise,
or signs of imminent mechanical failure, stop normal operation and escalate according to
the plant emergency procedure.
