FACTORYZERO TEST DOCUMENT — TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

SLIGHT / DEVELOPING CONDITION
If vibration rises above the normal baseline but remains below 7 mm/s, inspect:
- bearing lubrication and condition
- coupling alignment
- mounting bolts
- driven load
- recent maintenance history

If current rises toward 30 A, check:
- mechanical load
- phase balance
- electrical connections
- motor cooling
- bearing/mechanical resistance

SERIOUS CONDITION
If vibration exceeds 7 mm/s and temperature exceeds 85 °C while current exceeds 30 A,
treat the combination as a serious condition requiring prompt controlled intervention.

Possible causes:
- bearing degradation
- shaft/coupling misalignment
- mechanical overload
- electrical imbalance
- cooling problem
- combined mechanical/electrical deterioration

The AI diagnosis is a hypothesis, not a confirmed physical fault. Confirm using
approved inspection and measurement procedures.
