# Agents
## Sensor
Explains deterministic telemetry.
## Electrical Engineer
Investigates electrical causes such as overload, imbalance and supply issues.
## Mechanical Engineer
Investigates bearing, alignment, vibration, imbalance, friction and lubrication possibilities.
## Evidence
Checks supplied documents and citations.
## Maintenance Planner
Turns verified findings into an inspection-first plan.
## Plant Manager
Explains scheduling and production impact from deterministic inputs.

All agents must distinguish observation, hypothesis, evidence, recommendation and uncertainty.
